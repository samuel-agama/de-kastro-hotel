Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 21, 2026

At De Kastro Hotel Limited, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled securely and responsibly. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide when using our website or booking our services.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us to book a room or reserve our event space, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Full name and contact details (phone number, email address).
  • Booking details, including arrival and departure dates.
  • Payment information (processed securely at our front desk or via authorized banking channels).

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used to provide and improve our hospitality services. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Process your room reservations and event bookings.
  • Communicate with you regarding your stay, via phone calls or WhatsApp.
  • Respond to your inquiries and customer service requests.
  • Maintain internal record keeping for accounting and legal compliance.

3. Data Security

We are committed to ensuring that your information is secure. In order to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure, we have put in place suitable physical, electronic, and managerial procedures to safeguard and secure the information we collect directly from you.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to other websites (such as Google Maps or WhatsApp). Once you have used these links to leave our site, you should note that we do not have any control over those third-party websites. Therefore, we cannot be responsible for the protection and privacy of any information which you provide whilst visiting such sites.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us directly at our front desk or call +234 806 291 1356.
