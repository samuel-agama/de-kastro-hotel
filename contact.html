Frequently Asked Questions

How far is De Kastro Hotel from RCCG City Camp?
We are located directly opposite RCCG City Camp at 42/44 Redemption Way, Mowe. It is just a short walk or a very quick drive to the camp gates, allowing you to bypass heavy transit traffic.
How do I book a room?
We handle all bookings directly to ensure you get the best room for your needs. You can call our front desk at +234 806 291 1356 or chat with us on WhatsApp to confirm availability and lock in your rates.
How much are your rooms?
Our room rates are per night: Economy Room ₦30,000, Standard Room ₦35,000, Deluxe Room ₦45,000, Executive Room ₦55,000 and Double Bed Deluxe Room ₦65,000. Call or WhatsApp us on +234 806 291 1356 to confirm availability.
Is breakfast included?
Complimentary breakfast is available exclusively to guests staying in the Deluxe, Executive and Double Bed Deluxe rooms. It is served daily from 7:00 AM to 9:00 AM and ends promptly at 9:00 AM.
What time is check-out?
Check-out is on or before 12:00 noon. Any stay beyond the official check-out time is regarded as a new booking and attracts a fresh room charge.
How do I pay for my booking?
Please make all payments only to the hotel's official account, listed on our Terms & Conditions page, and present your payment confirmation at the reception for verification.
Do you have reliable power and security?
Absolutely. We provide 24/7 uninterrupted power, highly secure on-site parking, and round-the-clock security personnel to ensure our guests have a comfortable and safe stay.
What kind of events can I host in your hall?
Our event hall is perfect for wedding receptions, milestone birthdays, corporate meetings, seminars, and social gatherings. We offer flexible seating capacities and allow external decorators and caterers.
